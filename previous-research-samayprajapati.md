# Previous Research



Previous research on the use of technology and artificial intelligence can be broken up into three groups that can explain why and how students are relying on AI tools. The first group dives specifically into students in education and their trust, psychological needs, and the learning models that creates AI dependency. Digging deeper into this, researchers have explored how relying on generative AI tools affects the student's habits, critical thinking skills, and their creativity when problem solving throughout their coursework. The second group builds on this idea by studying the reliance on these platforms as well as digital vulnerability. This shows how users are able to form these functional and psychological habits throughout their daily lives. The third group looks at how people work alongside with AI systems, personal decision making styles, the perception of bias and what fairness is, and then crucial role of human oversight and the part it plays when working with AI systems. Together, these three groups of research show that even though AI is able to assist with everyday tasks, relying on them too heavily can alter the way individuals think, make decisions, and learn independently.
